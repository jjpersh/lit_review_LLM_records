## 2. Eligibility
Yes. A generative LLM (DeepSeek-V4-Flash for the headline results, Nemotron-3-Super-120B for the cross-family runs) acts as a supervisory controller. Every 5 minutes it writes composition setpoints to a fixed regulatory layer (linear MPC or PID) on a dynamic simulation of Skogestad's Column A, a standard distillation benchmark. This is a process system. The paper has three tested architectures, all simulated. Core.

## 3. What was built, in plain words
The plant is a simulated 40-stage binary distillation column (Skogestad "Column A", LV configuration: reflux L and boilup V are the manipulated variables). It is integrated in Python with scipy over 82 states (stage compositions and holdups). There is no hardware.

Two layers control the column. The regulatory layer runs every 1–5 s and moves L and V continuously. Condenser and reboiler levels are held by proportional controllers. The composition loops are run either by relay-tuned decentralized PID or by a linear MPC (do-mpc/CasADi, re-linearized per operating point, move-penalty weight ×100 as the "Pareto reference"). The supervisory layer runs every 5 min and sets the distillate and bottoms composition setpoints (yD, xB). It can be one of four things:
- C0: fixed operator setpoints over PID
- C1: linear MPC
- C2: the LLM agent over the MPC backend
- C3: C2 plus a safety gate

The agent is an Observer → Optimizer → Critic loop written in plain Python:
- **Observer** (deterministic): packs compositions, manipulated variables (MVs), running IAE and feed conditions into a structured prompt.
- **Optimizer** (the only LLM call): returns JSON with a yD target, an xB target and a rationale.
- **Critic** (rule-based): checks physical bounds and that the yD target exceeds the xB target. It allows at most two revisions, which run with chain-of-thought enabled. If the revisions run out, it re-emits the last accepted target, or aborts the run on the first cycle.

Accepted targets go through a rate limiter (ramp bound) into the regulatory layer. In C3, the safety gate checks every proposal before it executes. The gate deep-copies the plant state, simulates the twin 30 min ahead under the proposed setpoints, and tests nine pinned constraints. A violation in the first 5 min is flagged as "fast-fail". On any violation the gate blocks the proposal, substitutes the last accepted target, and logs the margins.

Model settings are pinned by a design of experiments. DeepSeek-V4-Flash ran at T=1.0 with reasoning effectively always on. Nemotron ran at T=0.3, top_p 0.95. Neither model was fine-tuned. The LLM never touches actuators.

## 4. Each tested setup
**Setup A: C2, ungated LLM supervisor over the linear-MPC backend** (headline, DeepSeek-V4-Flash).
- Grid: pre-registered 16 off-nominal operating points (F ∈ {0.8, 0.9, 1.1, 1.2} × zF ∈ {0.45, 0.475, 0.525, 0.55}).
- Two sub-metrics: target acquisition (start off-nominal from nominal MVs) and disturbance rejection (on-spec pre-staged start).
- Scenarios: F ±20 %, zF ±10 %, and a setpoint arm that in practice was an undisturbed hold. Disturbances step at t=5 min; each run is 60 min (12 cycles). N=10 seeds per cell.
- Comparators:
  - C1: linear MPC with a Pareto sweep of the move-penalty weight, ×100 variant as the reference (tuned).
  - C0: PID tuned by relay identification and Tyreus–Luyben, chosen by a pre-registered tuning shootout (tuned).
- Metrics: P95 IAE ratio C2/C1 with bootstrap CIs, per-scenario IAE, failure counts.
- Varied:
  - LLM family: a full Nemotron-3-Super 1600-cell sweep; Nemotron-3-Ultra and GLM-5.2 failed the JSON protocol.
  - Prompt: a v1 versus v2 disambiguation sensitivity test, 30 cells.
  - Sampling: a temperature/top_p/reasoning DoE, 225 runs.
- Earlier model selection: a 49B model failed on multi-step coherence.

**Setup B: C3, LLM supervisor plus counterfactual safety gate over the MPC backend.**
- Grid: 4 operating points (nominal; attractor (1.2, 0.55); control 1 (1.2, 0.45); control 2 (0.8, 0.55)) × 5 scenarios × 10 seeds, 250 cells including the C2 nominal companion.
- Earlier work: a first 40-cell gate evaluation and four case studies.
- Comparators: C2 (gate isolated), with C1 (≈0.38 IAE scale) and C0/C1 nominal point references.
- Metrics: canonical IAE, P95, Cohen's d, intercept and detection rates, block taxonomy, peak deviation.

**Setup C: C2_pid, the ungated agent directly over the unmodified PID layer (no MPC).**
- Nominal operating point only, 5 scenarios × 10 seeds = 50 cells, reported descriptively.
- Comparators: C0, C1 and C2.

## 5. Results
- **Target acquisition: the agent beats tuned MPC.** p.10: "C2 beats the Pareto-reference C1 by 2.77× on the full grid (P95 IAE 58.55 vs 162.08)". The ratio C2/C1 is 0.361 at the upper CI (0.351 point), Table 1. Lower is better. The denominator is the 16 operating points × 10 seeds. This is the paper's case of the LLM beating a non-LLM alternative: off-nominal target acquisition against linear MPC only.
- **Disturbance rejection: the agent loses to MPC.** p.10: "C1 beats C2 at an upper-CI ratio of 16.03 full-grid (10.18 at the point estimate; P95 IAE 3.90 vs 0.38)".
- **Cross-family.** p.22: "Super C2/C1 ≈ 1.69 full grid, 1.95 agent-feasible". Disturbance rejection still fails. Super's target-acquisition half collapsed (33/800 done), and 914/1600 cells failed plant-side (p.10).
- **JSON protocol operability.** p.22: "Super collapses that half 767/800 against ≈ 416/800 for V4-Flash". This counts protocol or plant failures across cells.
- **Gate containment.** p.11: "C3 canonical-IAE median is 0.486 [0.401, 0.642] against a C2 baseline of 6.401". Cohen's d = −1.44; P95 drops from 11.464 to 0.774. Lower is better.
- **Follower incidence and the prompt fix.** p.18: "the incidence drops from 6/10 to 0/10 (... Fisher exact p = 0.011)". The denominator is seeds at the attractor cell under v1 versus v2. The "passive follower" is the agent tracking the drifting plant instead of holding the specification.
- **Block taxonomy.** p.19: "590 blocks were recorded, and 534 of them carry the y D upper 0.99 trigger". By effect, the 590 blocks are 318 operationally effective (an off-spec proposal replaced), 221 no-ops and 51 run-fatal (p.15). The 590 come from 1,839 gated proposals (p.15).
- **Gate rates.** The intercept rate is 1.000 [1.000, 1.000] (p.15). The author says this is degenerate by construction: it audits log consistency, not detection skill. The onset false negatives (case cs04) fall outside this ground truth.
- **Control 2 made inoperable.** p.12: "all 50 cells are run-fatal at cycle 0". A zero-margin specification sits on a safety bound.
- **The gate costs nothing at nominal, on IAE.** p.14: "C3's IAE is equal to or lower than C2's (F − 20 %: 0.061 vs 0.198". At the nominal point the v1 agent's proposals actively hurt, and the gate removes them.
- **No recovery.** p.11: no cell of either configuration returns within a 10⁻³ band in 60 min.
- **Agent over PID is worse than PID alone.** p.23: "C2 pid ... below C0 on every disturbance arm". C2_pid is worse than C2 over MPC by factors of 2.8–37×. It was operable in 50/50 cells.
- **Latency.** Fig. 5 (p.14, from the page image), 600 cycles each:
  - C2: median 15.3 s, IQR 11.3–21.0 s, max 278 s
  - C3: median 18.6 s, IQR 15.2–23.3 s, max 63 s
  - C0/C1: 0.34 s or less
- **DoE confirmation run (Nemotron).** Mean canonical IAE 5.75×10⁻⁷ at the nominal baseline, with all 10 seeds identical (p.6).

## 7. Terms to define
- Column A: Skogestad's 40-stage binary distillation benchmark model.
- LV configuration: reflux L and boilup V are the composition manipulated variables. The configuration is strongly coupled (RGA ≈ 36).
- Target acquisition: driving the column from an off-nominal start with nominal MVs to the specification.
- Disturbance rejection: holding the specification after a feed step from an on-spec start.
- Pareto reference (×100): the MPC move-penalty weight with the best off-nominal IAE that still beats PID at nominal.
- Counterfactual (forked-twin) gate: a copy of the plant simulated 30 min ahead under the proposal and checked against nine constraints.
- Fast-fail: a gate violation that occurs within the first 5 min of the fork.
- Passive-follower attractor: the LLM re-reads the disturbed plant state as the new target and walks its setpoints away from the specification.
- Spec-on-bound: the operating specification (yD = 0.99) equals the safety bound, so small settling overshoot triggers blocks.
- Escalate / CriticLoopLimitError: re-emit the last accepted target, or abort, after two failed Critic revisions.
- ε tolerance: a 10⁻⁶ float-noise tolerance on the signed constraint margin.
- Outcome class B: the agent wins an off-nominal sub-metric by 1.5× against MPC, with bootstrap-CI separation.
