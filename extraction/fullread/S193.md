## 2. Eligibility
Eligible. Open-weight LLMs act as a judge or tuner for decentralized PI controllers on simulated process plants. In the main benchmark the LLM ranks two candidate PI gain sets, a judgment meant to feed controller selection (advisory). In the P2 ablation the LLM proposes PI gains, which are run directly in the closed-loop quadruple-tank simulator, with optional feedback of the measured cost. All plants are process systems or standard process benchmarks: the quadruple tank, the PC-Gym series CSTR with recycle, and a synthetic 3×3 cyclic plant. This is an offline benchmark, not real-time control. Recommended tier: core.

## 3. What was built, in plain words
There are three simulated MIMO plants, each run under decentralized position-form PI loops with saturation:
- a 2×2 non-minimum-phase quadruple tank, where pump voltages control levels;
- a 2×2 two-CSTR series reactor with recycle, where coolant temperatures control outlet concentrations and process gains are negative;
- a synthetic discrete 3×3 plant with dominant off-diagonal (cyclic) coupling.

The PI controllers always move the actuators, and actuation is continuous and saturated. For each plant, 320 PI gain vectors were sampled log-uniformly, and each closed loop was simulated to get its IAE. These IAE values are the ground truth.

The LLM sits outside the loop as an evaluator. It receives a qualitative plant description (coupling directions, gain sign, actuator range, conflicting setpoints) and two gain sets. It returns a forced choice (`FINAL: A/B`) for which set gives lower IAE. Secondary formats ask whether IAE goes up or down when one gain is changed, and whether a controller is good or poor relative to the pool median. A fixed parser reads the answer, and it is scored against the simulator label. Nothing reaches a plant.

Interventions on matched items:
- P3 adds the full equations (E) or 14-point sampled trajectories (T).
- P4 adds the trajectories, trajectories with the A/B values swapped (X), or a "scaffold" (S) that gives per-channel trapezoidal error integrals without totals.

In the P2 tuning ablation the LLM proposes 8 PI gain sets for the tank. Each set is simulated and scored with J = IAE + 0.75·TV. In the feedback cells, earlier gains and their J values are returned to the model before the next proposal. The best valid J is the outcome.

Models are Qwen3-14B, Qwen2.5-14B, Qwen2.5-7B and Llama-3.1-8B (bf16, single 48 GB GPU), plus Qwen2.5-32B 4-bit. The models get only prompts: no fine-tuning and no tools. Decoding is greedy for the probes and temperature 0.9 for P2.

## 4. Each tested setup
**Setups A–C: pairwise PI-gain ranking benchmark, one per plant (tank, reactor, cyclic).**
- Items: 100 pairwise items for the tank, 74 for the reactor and 100 for the cyclic plant, plus 40 direction and 40 tracking-quality items per plant, and 12–16 declarative QA items (E1). Each set is run once per checkpoint with greedy decoding.
- Difficulty bins use the IAE ratio. The reactor has no "easy" pairs.
- P0 re-runs the tank items after correcting the controller-form metadata.
- P1 regenerates the controller pools with two sampling designs × 3 seeds, 50 items each.
- P3 uses 50 matched items per plant under Q/E/T.
- P4 uses 271 items under Q/T/X/S.
- A perturbation audit uses 10 seeds combining parameter multipliers (±15% clip), measurement noise, a one-step delay and two state disturbances. The labels are recomputed and the existing Q choices rescored; no new inference is run.
- A metric audit compares the IAE labels with ISE, peak error and other metrics.
- Comparators: chance (50%), simple gain-magnitude rules, and a non-LLM numerical oracle (trapezoidal integration of the 14 displayed rows). There is no tuned-controller comparator, because the task is ranking.
- Metrics: raw, balanced and parsed accuracy; parse coverage; choice-flip rate; agreement with the rules.
- What was varied: checkpoint, information package, sampling design, reasoning prompt (60-item tank check), and model size (32B 4-bit).

**Setup D: LLM gain-proposal tuning ablation (P2), tank only.**
- Design: 2×2 cells, knowledge K0/K1 × feedback F0/F1.
- Runs: 10 generation seeds × 8 proposals per cell, for 4 checkpoints (1280 proposals).
- Metric: best penalized cost J (lower is better), reported as mean ± SD, with bootstrap CIs.
- No non-LLM tuner comparator, and nominal simulation only.

## 5. Results
- **Declarative knowledge vs ranking.** E1 is 98–100% pooled by plant. Pairwise ranking is "46.8% for the quadruple-tank, 31.8% for the reactor, and 53.2% for the 3 × 3 plant" (p.11). This is pooled raw accuracy over 4 checkpoints × items, with 50% chance.
- **Per-checkpoint pairwise ranges** (p.12):
  - tank 33–58%;
  - reactor 16.2–59.5%; "Qwen2.5-14B, p = 2.9 × 10−9" below chance (p.12);
  - cyclic 43–63%.
- **P0 (tank, corrected metadata).** "accuracy remains between 46% and 54%" (p.13).
- **P3 (full equations or 14-point trajectories).** "46.8% (281/600), 51.2% (307/600), and 51.0% (306/600)" for Q/E/T (p.13). All 12 T−Q intervals include zero, and Holm-adjusted p = 1.0. The numerical oracle recovers 145/150 (96.7%) of labels from the same rows (p.8).
- **P4 swap test.** "the descriptive choice-flip rate is only 9.5%" (p.14). The denominator is 1062 parsed T/X pairs. A model that followed the evidence would flip 100%.
- **P4 scaffold.** "Evidence-label accuracy is 51.1% (554/1084) for T but 88.1% (955/1084) for S" (p.14). By checkpoint, S is 99.6/82.7/84.5/85.6%, and 11 of 12 contrasts survive Holm correction.
- **Perturbation.** "88.7% of the 2710 seed–item labels retain their nominal ordering" (p.15). Rescored LLM accuracy under perturbation is 41.0–49.7%.
- **Metric audit.** IAE labels agree with ISE on 95.2% of 271 pairs and with peak error on 71.2% (p.15).
- **Gain-magnitude bias.** Raw agreement with "choose the smaller controller" is 71.2/70.3/60.2% (tank/reactor/cyclic). That rule is correct on only 37.0/9.5/69.0% of probe pairs (p.16).
- **Reasoning prompt (60 tank items).** "The four-checkpoint descriptive mean changes from 50% to 44%" (p.19).
- **32B 4-bit model.** "55%, 50%, and 49% on the tank, reactor, and 3 × 3 pairwise probes" (p.20).
- **P2 tuning.** Feedback gave lower mean best J "in only two of eight checkpoint–knowledge contrasts" (p.18). Best-J means range from 14.958 to 18.606 (Table 9, p.18). For example, Qwen2.5-14B K0: ΔJ = +1.736 with feedback.
- **Latency.** Median request times are 0.18–0.64 s per checkpoint (p.27).
- **LLM vs non-LLM.** The paper does not show the LLM beating any non-LLM alternative. The simple numerical oracle (96.7%) far exceeds the raw LLM ranking (around 47–51%). P2 has no non-LLM tuning comparator.

## 7. Terms to define
- **IAE (integrated absolute error):** the time integral of |setpoint − output|, summed over loops. Here it is the ground-truth label (lower is better).
- **TV / TVP2:** total variation of the pump command, used as a control-effort penalty in J = IAE + 0.75·TV.
- **Position-form PI with integral rollback:** the PI output is computed from the accumulated integral, and the last integral increment is undone when the actuator saturates (anti-windup).
- **E1:** the declarative control-theory multiple-choice question set.
- **E2 pairwise:** "which of two gain sets gives lower IAE?"
- **P0 / P1 / P2 / P3 / P4:**
  - P0: metadata-correction rerun;
  - P1: pool resampling;
  - P2: knowledge × feedback tuning ablation;
  - P3: equations/trajectories intervention;
  - P4: trajectory-swap and scaffold audit.
- **Q / E / T / X / S:**
  - Q: qualitative description;
  - E: full equations;
  - T: 14-point trajectories;
  - X: trajectories with A/B values swapped;
  - S: per-channel error-integral scaffold.
- **Evidence label:** the ranking implied by trapezoidal integration of the displayed samples, as opposed to the full-simulation label.
- **Choice-flip rate:** the fraction of items whose answer changes when the displayed A/B trajectories are swapped.
- **Balanced accuracy:** the mean recall over the A and B labels.
- **Holm correction:** a step-down multiple-testing adjustment.
- **McNemar test:** a paired test on discordant item outcomes.
- **Gain-magnitude rule (raw / percentile-normalized):** prefer the gain set with the smaller summed |gain|, or the smaller summed pool percentile of |gain|.
- **Parse coverage:** the share of outputs containing a valid FINAL token.
