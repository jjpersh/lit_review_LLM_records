## 2. Why it is context
The authors label it a perspective, "a practitioner's perspective rather than a performance study" (p.2), and the four scenarios are "representative composites... The specifics are constructed" (p.4); the appendix investigation is also invented ("The series, procedure text, and earlier events are invented for illustration", p.9). They state the system runs live in an advisory role on production bioreactors (p.2, p.5), but they report no results of their own: no counts, rates, accuracy, timing or comparison (Fig. 2 is "Not a benchmark", p.4). The LLM also has no control role: it "issues no control action" (p.3), has read-only access (p.6), and only drafts monitoring and alarm-interpretation briefs, so no control layer is acted on, advised, tuned or connected in a tested setup.

## 3. Background use
- **Layered, bounded architecture for LLMs in GMP operations.** A fixed, versioned deterministic evidence layer records events and baseline severity. An LLM reasoning layer reads a structured "evidence contract", calls read-only Model Context Protocol tools (historian data, quality records, procedures, charts), and drafts a cited advisory operator brief. A human holds disposition (pp.2-3, pp.5-7, Figs. 1, 3, 4). The authors explicitly ground this in IEC-style separation of the safety function from basic control, and in Layer of Protection Analysis (p.3).
- **Safeguard design principles that a review can cite as stated practice (not as tested evidence).** The LLM cannot change the recorded event or its severity. Disagreements are logged, not overwritten. High-severity detections reach the operator whatever the LLM ranks them ("hard floor"). If the LLM fails, baseline severity sets priority. A deterministic check makes every cited fact resolve to the record, and a separate-context model review scores the assessment. Only human-reviewed verdicts become precedent (pp.3, 5-6). The paper also cites the April 2026 FDA cGMP warning letter on AI-generated records without quality-unit review (p.5).
- **Proposed evaluation agenda and autonomy staging.** Evaluate whole cases, comparing fixed retrieval, single-pass interpretation and adaptive investigation. Use missed concerns, unnecessary escalations, timeliness, unsupported claims and repeat consistency as metrics, with development and assessment cases kept separate (pp.8). Autonomy is placed in Parasuraman-style terms: information acquisition and analysis only. "Action on the process remains the domain of validated, deterministic control" (p.8). Models are assigned per task in configuration: commercial, open-weight or fine-tuned in-house (p.6).
